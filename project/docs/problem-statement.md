# Problem Statement

College students frequently need help with subjects, tools, or skills but struggle to find peers who can assist them. Traditional academic support systems (tutors, office hours) are limited in availability and scope. Meanwhile, many students possess valuable skills they could share but lack a platform to offer them.

**SkillBridge** solves this by creating a peer-to-peer skill exchange platform where students can:
1. Offer skills they know to other students
2. Create help requests when they need assistance
3. Browse and search for relevant skills
4. Get matched with students who can help
5. Submit feedback after receiving help

This creates a self-sustaining learning community within the campus.
